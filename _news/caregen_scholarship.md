I am honored to announce that I have been selected as a recipient of the **Caregen Jung Yong-ji Scholarship** (케어젠정용지장학금) in the Department of Convergent Bioscience at **Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU)**.

This scholarship recognizes students with high research potential and academic excellence in convergent biotechnology. I am grateful for this support and look forward to continuing my research in computational biology and deep learning.

The official announcement can be found [here](https://skb.skku.edu/gene/community/under_notice.do?mode=view&articleNo=213080&article.offset=20&articleLimit=10).
